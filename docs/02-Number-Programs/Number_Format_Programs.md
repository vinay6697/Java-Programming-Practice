	1)Fibonacci series: if the number formed by the sum of previous two numbers
		then we call such numbers as fibonacci series
		
		eg: 1
		previous numbers
		 0 ,1==>0+1=1
		 1 ,1==>1+1=2
		 2 ,1==>1+2=3
		 3 ,2==>2+3=5
		 
	2)Spy Number: If the sum of the digits in the given number and 
		product of the digits in the given number are same 
		then such numbers are called Spy Numbers
		
		eg: 123
		sum of 123		==>1+2+3=6
		product of 123	==> 1*2*3=6	
		
		since sum==product it is a spy number
		
		other spy  numbers are 132, 6,1124,22
		
		
	3) Prime Number: If the number is divisible only by 1 and the number itself is called 
			as prime number
			
			eg: 3 ==> it can be divisible only by 1 and 3
			   11 ==> it can be divisible only by 1 and 11
			   
			   other prime numbers are
			   23,29,31,37 etc
	
	4) Palindrome number
		If the number and the reverse of the number is same
		then we call such numbers as Palindrome numbers
		
		eg:121 where reverse of 121 is also 121
		other examples of palindrome numbers are
		1331,99999,13231,2,8
		
	5) Prime Palindrome: If the number is both palindrome and prime number
		then such numbers are called prime palindrome number
		eg: 131
		the palindrome of the number is 131 only
		and it is divisible by only 1 and the number itself
		
		other Prime Palindrome numbers are
		
		11,101,131,151,181,191,313,353,373 etc
		
	6)Xylem and Phloem Number: If the sum of mean numbers and sum of extreme numbers
		are equal then such numbers are called Xylem numbers
		if they are not equal then such numbers are called Phloem numbers
		
		eg:  1223
			1 + 3 =4
			2 + 2 =4
			so they are xylem numbers
			
			1532
			1 + 2=3
			5 + 3=8
			so they are phloem numbers 
			
		other examples of Xylem and Phloem number
		 123,12452,67876,
		 
	7)Perfect Number: A positive integer that is equal to the sum of its proper divisors excluding the number itself
		eg : 6 The Divisors are 1,2,3 not 6 
		where 1+2+3=6
		
		other perfect numbers are 
		28,496,8128
		
		Here the sum of the divisors are  equal to the number itself
		
		
	8)Sunny Number: The number such that adding 1 to it should become a
		perfect square.
		
		eg 1: 3
		where 3+1=4 i.e 2*2=4
		so 3 is a sunny number
		
		eg 2: 8
		where 8+1=9 i.e 3*3=9
		so 8 is sunny number
		
		other sunny  numbers are
		 3, 8, 15, 24, 35, 48, 63
		 
	9)Buzz Number :If the number either divisible by 7 or the number ends with
		the digit 7
		
		eg: 7,49,56,14,
		
		
	10)Strong number : A positive integer whose sum of factorial of digits is equal to 
				the Original number itself
				
	eg:145  1! + 4! + 5!
			1  + 4*3*2*1 + 5*4*3*2*1 
			1  + 24		 + 120
			145
			
			other Strong numbers  1,2,145,40585 etc

	 to the power of the number of digits
	 
	 eg 371    the no of digits is 3
	 sum of the digits each raised to the power of no of digits
	 
	 3**3 + 7**3 + 1**1

	12)Automorphic Number: If the square of the given number ends with the number
		then it is called as Automorphic number
		
		eg: 	5 square of 5 is=25
				6 square of 6 is=36
				25 square of 25 is =625
				
		other examples of Automorphic numbers are 
		76--> 5776,
		376--->1,41,376

		9376---->8,79,09,376
	
	13) Neon number: If the square of the number and the sum of each digit is equal to the given number
					then we call such numbers as Neon  numbers

		eg :  9
			square 0f 9 =81
			= 8 + 1 = 9
			= 9==9
	14) Diserium Number: a number where the sum of its digits raised to the power of their respective 1-based positions (from 	left to right) equals the number itself
		
		e.g :
			 89: 8¹ + 9² = 8 + 81 = 89 (It is a Disarium number)
			 135: 1¹ + 3² + 5³ = 1 + 9 + 125 = 135 (It is a Disarium number)
			 80: 8¹ + 0² = 8 + 0 = 8 (Not a Disarium number)

	16) Tech Number: if the first half of the number and the second half of the number is added and
					squared if the resultant value equals to the given number such numbers are called 
					Tech Number
		e.g : 2025
			20 + 25=45
			45 square is 2025

			such numbers are called Tech Numbers
		
	other tech numbers are :81, 2025 ,3025, 9801

	17) Duck Number : if a given number doesn't start with 0 but has at least one 0 in the number in any position then they are called 
					Duck Number.
	eg:
		0100-->not a duck number because the number starts with 0
		100--> Duck number because it has 0 present inside it
	
	18) Pronic Number: if a number formed by the product of two consecutive numbers are called Pronic numbers
	e.g :
		2*3=6;
		3*4=12
		4*5=20
		5*6=30
		6*7=42

	19) GCD of Two Numbers:  if a given two numbers are divisible by the highest divisors then it is called the GCD of 
							two numbers
		e.g: 12 and 20
		12 is divisible by 1,2,3,4,6,12
		20 is divisible by 1,2,4,5,10,20
		
	now the common factors among them are 1,2,4
	and the highest them are 4
