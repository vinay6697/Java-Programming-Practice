Find the GCD (Greatest Common Divisor) of Two Numbers

EXPLANATION:
Write a Java program to find the Greatest Common Divisor (GCD) of two given numbers.

The GCD of two numbers is the largest positive integer that divides both numbers without leaving a remainder.

For example:
The GCD of 12 and 18 is 6 because 6 is the greatest number that divides both 12 and 18 exactly.
e.g: 12 and 20
		12 is divisible by 1,2,3,4,6,12
		20 is divisible by 1,2,4,5,10,20
		

The program should accept two integers as input, calculate their GCD, and display the result.

CONSTRAINTS:
1. The input must consist of two non-negative integers.
2. Both numbers must be valid integers.
3. The GCD must be the greatest positive common divisor of both numbers.
4. If one number is zero, the GCD is the other number (provided it is non-zero).
5. The GCD of 0 and 0 is undefined.

TEST CASES:

Test Case 1:
Input:
12 18
Output: 6

Test Case 2:
Input:
24 36
Output: 12

Test Case 3:
Input:
15 25
Output: 5

Test Case 4:
Input:
7 13
Output: 1

Test Case 5:
Input:
0 10
Output: 10
