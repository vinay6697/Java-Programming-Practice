Find the LCM of Two Numbers

 EXPLANATION:
Given two positive integers, find their Least Common Multiple (LCM).

The LCM of two numbers is the smallest positive number that is exactly divisible by both numbers.

Example:
Input: 12, 18
Output: 36

Explanation:
36 is the smallest positive number that is divisible by both 12 and 18.

 CONSTRAINTS:
1. Both input numbers must be positive integers.
2. The two numbers can be equal.
3. The numbers can be different.
4. The LCM must be a positive integer.

TEST CASES:

Test Case 1:
Input:
12
18

Output:
36
